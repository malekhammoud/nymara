# Project Overview: Nymara

## What is Nymara?
Nymara is a climate-tech infrastructure initiative focused on urban resilience. It specializes in the development of **hyper-porous road systems** integrated with **advanced nano-engineered coatings**. By reimagining traditional asphalt and concrete surfaces as active drainage systems, Nymara aims to mitigate the catastrophic impacts of urban flooding and the "heat island" effect.

---

## The Problem: Rising Urban Vulnerability
The world is expanding into the path of rising waters at an unsustainable rate. 

* **Rapid Encroachment:** Between 1985 and 2015, urban land within floodplains grew by **2.5% annually**.
* **Population Surge:** The number of people living in flood-prone urban zones has increased by **122 million**.
* **Property Risk:** There are currently over **1.3 million high-risk properties** globally.
* **The Zone Fallacy:** Nearly **60% of urban flood damages** occur outside of officially designated flood zones, proving that traditional infrastructure is failing to adapt to modern weather patterns.
* **The Concrete Jungle Effect:** Conventional non-porous surfaces prevent groundwater recharge and accelerate runoff, leading to flash floods and overwhelmed sewage systems.

---

## The Solution: Hyper-Porous Infrastructure
Nymara offers a **10X Impact** solution by transforming the most common urban surface—the road—into a tool for flood management.

### Key Technology:
1.  **Hyper-Porous Substrates:** Unlike traditional pavement, Nymara’s roads allow water to pass through the surface at an accelerated rate, preventing pooling and flash runoff.
2.  **Nano-Engineered Coatings:** These specialized coatings enhance the structural integrity of the porous material, ensuring durability under heavy traffic loads while preventing clogging from sediment and pollutants.
3.  **Smart Drainage Integration:** The system acts as a first-line defense, filtering and slowing down water before it enters municipal systems or replenishing local aquifers directly.

---

## Why It Matters (The Impact)
* **Flood Mitigation:** Reduces the peak flow of water during extreme rain events, protecting properties located both inside and outside traditional flood zones.
* **Urban Cooling:** Porous materials and nano-coatings help dissipate heat more effectively than standard blacktop asphalt.
* **Economic Resilience:** By reducing the $100B+ annual global cost of flood damage, Nymara helps cities remain habitable and insurable.
* **Sustainability:** Facilitates natural water cycles within urban environments, promoting healthier soil and groundwater levels.
